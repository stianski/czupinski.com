About

I have spent about seven years in cybersecurity. Today I am a Senior Consultant in GRC; before that I was an Information Systems Security Officer in the insurance sector and an IT Security Specialist on public-sector projects.

My work sits in the part of security that lives in processes and documents: an ISMS built on ISO/IEC 27001, risk management, third-party risk, audit, and regulatory requirements such as DORA and NIS2.

I work in Polish and English. I am currently preparing for the CISSP exam.

Services

What I can help with. We always agree the scope first, after a short conversation.

NIS2 / KSC gap analysis

A check of which requirements of the NIS2 Directive and the Polish KSC Act are already met and which are missing. The result is a list of gaps and a prioritised action plan.

Ask about this service

ISMS based on ISO/IEC 27001

Designing or tidying up an information security management system: scope, roles, risk methodology, policies and procedures. Also an internal audit ahead of certification.

Ask about this service

Third-party risk (TPRM)

A supplier assessment process: due diligence, security assessments, risk scoring and remediation tracking. Also in support of DORA requirements.

Ask about this service

Business continuity based on ISO 22301

Business continuity and recovery plans, response playbooks and exercises. Also an internal audit of the business continuity management system.

Ask about this service

SillyTavern

A private installation of SillyTavern, an interface for chatting with language models, on my own VPS. The service runs in Docker and is reachable only over a private Tailscale network.

A firewall blocks all traffic from the internet, I log in to the server with an SSH key only, security updates install automatically and a backup is taken every day. Images are generated by ComfyUI on my own computer, connected to the server over the same network.
